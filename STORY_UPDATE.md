# Luma's Garden — v0.3

Luma, the Hollow's last gardener, guides a tiny seed-carrying life toward the Root Devourer. Five optional golden shrines recover her memories and change the ending narration. Continue existing saves; no restart is required.

- Awakening Cavern: introductory three-lantern sequence.
- Fungal Grotto: interpret a seed / rain / sunlight clue.
- Sunken Ruins: grow to at least 4.375 m and rest on the weight seal for three seconds.
- Verdant Basin: complete a three-lantern courier route within 14 seconds.
- Ancient Nest: restore the night / dawn / day sequence.

Each shrine grants biomass, 45 Essence, healing, and a permanent journal entry. Read the journal from Pause. Incorrect sequences reset; timed challenges can be retried indefinitely. Partial shrine progress resets when changing chambers; completed memories persist through saves and death. Recover all five for the Garden Remembers epilogue.

Feeding streaks last four seconds and award 4% additional biomass per consecutive object, capped at 40%. New visual work includes smooth glossy slime with subtle surface movement, a floating lantern companion, gold shrine geometry, drifting instanced motes, and reduced emission washout. Motion settings affect the new animation.

Validation: headless combat/growth integration suite, five shrine solutions, incorrect order, spatial pad activation, insufficient size, timed failure/retry, duplicate reward protection, disk save roundtrip, reset, and ending selection. Browser checks cover desktop launch/gameplay and the scrollable journal at 390 x 844. Real mobile hardware, gamepad play, full human playthrough pacing, and subjective puzzle difficulty remain unverified.
